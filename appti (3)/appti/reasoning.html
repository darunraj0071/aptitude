Mastering Logical Reasoning for Campus Recruitment

Logical Reasoning is a decisive component in screening tests for top recruiters. It tests your problem-structuring abilities and structural rigor without relying on memorized formulas. Success relies on systematic diagramming, tree building, and truth elimination.

Diagramming & Setup Strategy

  • Family Trees: Draw generation levels vertically and use standardized symbols (+ for male, - for female, = for spouses).
  • Seating Arrangement: Start with definite statements before placing relative or conditional clues.
  • Direction Sense: Always sketch a 4-quadrant cross before plotting turns and displacements.

Deductive Speed Techniques

  • Alphabet Position Index: Use the EJOTY rule (5, 10, 15, 20, 25) for immediate letter numbering.
  • Venn Diagrams in Syllogisms: Draw minimal overlapping sets to test mandatory versus possible conclusions.
  • Timed Simulation: Test your agility under real exam pressure on our Mock Test Center.
