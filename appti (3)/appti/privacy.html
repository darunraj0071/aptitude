Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to VetriPathLearn ("we", "our", "us"). Your privacy is deeply important to us. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our placement preparation web portal (vetripathlearn.site) and Android mobile application. It outlines what information we collect, how it is protected, and your choices regarding data usage.

1. Information Collection

VetriPathLearn is designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not require account creation or mandatory login to access learning materials, practice tests, or coding modules.

  • Local Account & Progress Data: Your test scores, bookmarked questions, solved status, streak records, and preference settings are stored locally on your device via localStorage or IndexedDB.
  • Voluntary Contact Information: If you contact us via email or our contact form, we collect the name and email address provided to respond to your inquiry.
  • Non-Personal Technical Logs: Standard web browser technical data (IP address, browser type, operating system version, page access timestamps) may be automatically logged by our hosting infrastructure (Vercel) for maintenance and security monitoring.

2. Cookies & Storage Technologies

We utilize local browser storage mechanisms to enhance your experience:

  • Local Storage & Session Storage: Used to save your active theme preferences, exam state timer, and solved question counts.
  • Essential Cookies: Cookies necessary for session security and navigation integrity across pages.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense to support the ongoing development of free educational content.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymous telemetry regarding site traffic, page views, and user retention. This data helps us optimize performance and refine our curriculum topics. No personally identifiable information (PII) is transmitted to Google Analytics.

5. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All web traffic is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS). Since your study data resides on your own device, you maintain primary control over your learning data.

6. Third-Party Services

Our platform may contain links to external educational resources, documentation sites, or social media pages (GitHub, LinkedIn). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage users to read the privacy statements of any external site visited.

7. Children's Privacy

VetriPathLearn is designed for students preparing for campus placements, competitive exams, and technical interviews. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for deletion.
