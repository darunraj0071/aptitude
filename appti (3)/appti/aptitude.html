Campus Placement Aptitude Preparation Strategy

Quantitative Aptitude constitutes 30% to 50% of initial online assessment rounds conducted by leading IT service and product companies. Scoring well in quantitative aptitude requires not just solving questions correctly, but optimizing time per problem down to under 60 seconds through mental shortcuts and unit conversions.

Key Preparation Focus Areas

  • Speed Math & Estimation: Memorize squares up to 30, cubes up to 15, and fraction-to-percentage conversions.
  • Time & Work / Pipes: Use the LCM method instead of fractional arithmetic to solve combined rate problems.
  • Profit, Loss & Discount: Practice multiplier ratios to calculate net profit across multiple markups instantly.

Exam Day Best Practices

  • First Pass Triage: Answer high-confidence questions in Arithmetic and Number Systems before complex Geometry.
  • Option Elimination: Use last-digit properties and divisibility checks to eliminate wrong options without full calculation.
  • Timed Simulation: Take full-length tests on our Mock Test Simulator to build stamina.
