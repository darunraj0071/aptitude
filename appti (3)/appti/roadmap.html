The Complete 4-Stage Campus Placement Roadmap

A structured, step-by-step master roadmap designed to transition engineering and science graduates from absolute basics to final interview readiness over an 8-week structured timeline.

Weeks 01 – 02

Stage 1: Core Conceptual Foundations

The foundation stage focuses on core arithmetic, basic deductive reasoning, fundamental English grammar rules, and programming syntax familiarity.

Stage 1 Milestone Checklist
  • Quant: Number Systems, Divisibility rules, HCF/LCM, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio & Proportion.
  • Reasoning: Number Series, Alphabet Sequences (EJOTY), Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense.
  • Verbal: Parts of Speech, Subject-Verb Agreement, 12 Verb Tenses, Articles and Prepositions.
  • Coding: Python variables, loops, conditional statements, list manipulations, string slicing.
Weeks 03 – 04

Stage 2: Multi-Concept Problem Solving

Transition from single-step formulas to multi-step problem solving, analytical puzzle structuring, reading comprehension speed, and core data structures.

Stage 2 Milestone Checklist
  • Quant: Time & Work (LCM method), Pipes & Cisterns, Time-Speed-Distance, Trains/Boats, Simple & Compound Interest.
  • Reasoning: Blood Relations family trees, Syllogisms Venn deduction, Linear & Circular Seating Arrangements.
  • Verbal: Reading Comprehension questions-first technique, Para Jumbles mandatory pairs, Sentence Correction.
  • Coding: Hash Maps, Sets, Frequency counters, Two-Pointer algorithms, Big-O complexity analysis.
Weeks 05 – 06

Stage 3: Advanced Placement & Timed Drills

Tackle advanced combinatorics, probability, floor puzzles, and company-specific assessment patterns (TCS NQT, Infosys, Cognizant, Wipro).

Stage 3 Milestone Checklist
  • Quant: Permutation & Combination ($^nP_r, ^nC_r$), Probability, Data Interpretation (Pie/Bar charts).
  • Reasoning: Multi-attribute Floor Puzzles, Statement & Assumptions, Data Sufficiency.
  • Verbal: High-yield etymology roots, Idioms & Phrasal verbs, 4-point error spotting scans.
  • Coding: Classic placement algorithms: Two Sum, Kadane's Max Subarray, Valid Palindrome, Binary Search.
Weeks 07 – 08

Stage 4: Final Sprint & Mock Exam Simulation

Full-length timed mock examinations, bookmark review, speed triage calibration, and mock HR/Technical interview preparations.

Stage 4 Milestone Checklist
  • Mock Tests: Take 2 full-length 90-minute simulations weekly on the Mock Simulator.
  • Error Audit: Drill bookmarked challenging problems using the "Run Bookmarks Library" on the Practice Portal.
  • Speed Math: Daily 10-minute mental math drills for squares, cubes, and fraction percentages.

Roadmap & Study Planning FAQs

Frequently asked questions on tracking progress, daily time allocation, and roadmap completion verification.

How much time should I dedicate daily to placement preparation?
A balanced study routine requires 2 to 3 hours per day:
• 45 mins: Quantitative Aptitude (10–15 practice questions).
• 45 mins: Logical Reasoning or Verbal Ability (1–2 puzzles / RC passages).
• 45 mins: Coding & DSA (1–2 algorithm implementations).
• 15 mins: Error log review and saving bookmarked questions.
How do the interactive 30-Day Department Trackers work?
Click any department track above (Web Development, Python, DSA, Data Science, Core CS). Each day displays concept notes and hands-on tasks. When you finish a day's work, click "Verify & Complete" to take a concept verification check. Your progress is saved automatically to local storage and updates your completion percentage in real-time.
Can I study for aptitude and coding simultaneously?
Yes, and it is strongly recommended. Most corporate recruitment drives (such as TCS Digital and Infosys SP) test both aptitude and coding on the same day. Following our 4-stage roadmap ensures continuous, simultaneous growth across mathematics, logical reasoning, and programming without burning out.
